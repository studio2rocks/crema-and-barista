I have created Crema & Craft, an application to track daily coffees, calculate verified point scores, burn date-time stamps onto barista photos, and compete on a worldwide coffee database.
What Was Built:
Daily Coffee Tracker & Ledger:
Date Navigator: Shift between days, pick any calendar date, or jump to today.
Bento Summary Metrics: Real-time aggregation of today's total coffee points, cups consumed, average cup quality score, and your estimated world ranking.
Cup Ledger Cards: Detailed cards showing cup order (Cup #1, Cup #2), exact timestamp, drink name, roastery/shop, barista credit, cupping notes, and scoring factor badges.
Scoring Breakdown & Point Calculator:
Coffee Type Craft (15–30 pts): Siphon (30 pts), Pour Over (28 pts), Cortado/Gibraltar (25 pts), Flat White (24 pts), Double Espresso (23 pts), AeroPress (24 pts), Nitro Cold Brew (23 pts), Cappuccino (21 pts), Latte (18 pts), and more.
Cup Size & Ratio Factor: Single/Piccolo (1.1x ratio bonus), Double/Barista Standard (1.15x golden cup bonus), Standard (1.0x), Large (0.92x), and Triple (1.12x).
Location & Roastery Rating (1–10 scale 
 5–25 pts): Evaluates roastery pedigree, bean transparency, and atmosphere.
Barista Look & Craft (1–10 scale 
 6–30 pts): Evaluates latte art intricacy (swan, rosetta, tulip), microfoam gloss, cup cleanliness, and barista skill.
Coffee Shop Look & Aesthetic (1–10 scale 
 4–20 pts): Evaluates Nordic oak/concrete architectural aesthetic, foliage, and cafe ambiance.
Single-Origin Microlot Bonus (+5 pts).
Point Summing: Live preview updates instantaneously as you adjust sliders and options.
Date-Time Stamp & Barista Photo Upload:
Photo upload via file picker or camera, plus sample artisan coffee photos.
Canvas Stamping Engine: Burns an editorial date-time watermark onto the photo with the verified date, time, drink name, cafe name, city, and calculated score stamp (★ 98 PTS · CREMA & CRAFT VERIFIED).
Click to inspect the stamped photo in a full-screen lightbox or download it.
Worldwide Coffee Database & Global Competition:
Head-to-Head Comparison: Compares your scores against the World #1 Connoisseur (Yukihiro Tanaka, Tokyo) and the World Community Average across all four pillars.
Global Leaderboards: Today's Daily Cup Race (dynamically placing you based on today's logged cups) and All-Time Legends.
Filters & Search: Filter by region (Asia-Pacific, Europe, North America, Latin America) or search by connoisseur, city, or coffee shop.
Connoisseur Profiles: View signature brewing methods, home roasteries, and recent verified cups.
Live Global Activity Feed & Scoring Rubric:
Live Global Feed: Simulated real-time stream of cups being logged across Tokyo, Melbourne, Oslo, Milan, and Seattle, with an interactive "Cheers ☕" button.
Transparent Rubric & Sandbox: Interactive calculator to test any drink combination and view point yields.
